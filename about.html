Our Story

MISUS Refractories & Allied Industries is a manufacturer and supplier of high-quality refractory materials located in Barjora, Bankura, West Bengal. We specialize in producing refractory solutions used in high-temperature industrial operations such as steel manufacturing, blast furnaces, foundries, and other metallurgical processes.

Since our establishment in 2009, the organization has focused on delivering reliable refractory materials that provide durability, thermal resistance, and consistent performance. Our commitment to quality manufacturing and long-term customer satisfaction drives every product we make.

Our products are manufactured using carefully selected raw materials and controlled production processes to ensure stable chemical composition and reliable performance in demanding industrial environments.
