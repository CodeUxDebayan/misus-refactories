A Legacy of Quality

Misus Refractories And Allied Industries is a premier manufacturer of high-grade refractory products. Established with a vision to deliver unparalleled quality, we specialize in Castables, Blast Furnace Ramming Mass, and a comprehensive range of monolithic refractories.

Located in the industrial hub of Bankura, our advanced manufacturing facility allows us to process and formulate refractories that offer exceptional thermal stability, mechanical strength, and resistance to chemical attacks. We serve diverse sectors including steel, cement, power, and non-ferrous metals.
