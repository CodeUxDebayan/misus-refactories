Flagship Product

Castables

The MR-Crete range of conventional castables are generally usable for moderate and high heat applications. These are Calcium Aluminate bonded refractories for applications up to 1400°C. High Alumina conventional castables are made with alumina aggregate and high alumina refractory cement, suitable up to 1700°C. Low Cement Castables are available from 45% to 90% Al₂O₃ content, suitable up to 1800°C, with low porosity, better corrosion and erosion resistance, and good thermal shock resistance. Wide range of insulating castables available for both hot face and backup refractory lining.

Premium ramming mass designed specifically for blast furnace runners and hearths. Formulated for excellent resistance to corrosion from molten iron and slag, ensuring extended campaign life. NozCEOling Compound

Specially developed free-flowing compound used for filling slide gate nozzles in steel ladles. Guarantees high free-opening rates and effectively prevents steel penetration into the nozzle assembly. Formulated for consistent flow characteristics and excellent sintering behavior under service conditions to ensure safe steel-casting operations.

High Alumina Gunning

Designed for hot and cold gunning repair of various industrial furnaces and ladles. Features excellent adhesion to the substrate, negligible rebound loss during application, and sets rapidly to form a dense, strong monolithic structure. Ideal for maintaining and extending the life of existing refractory linings.
