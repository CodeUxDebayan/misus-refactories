Flagship Product

Castables

The MR-Crete range of conventional castables are generally usable for moderate and high heat applications. These are Calcium Aluminate bonded refractories for applications up to 1400°C. High Alumina conventional castables are made with alumina aggregate and high alumina refractory cement, suitable up to 1700°C. Low Cement Castables are available from 45% to 90% Al₂O₃ content, suitable up to 1800°C, with low porosity, better corrosion and erosion resistance, and good thermal shock resistance. Wide range of insulating castables available for both hot face and backup refractory lining.
